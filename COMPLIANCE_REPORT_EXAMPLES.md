## 📊 Example Report Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Strong Performer

**Current Data:**
- Current: 87.5% (Excellent)
- Highest: 95.2%
- Lowest: 81.8%
- Change: +8.3% (↑ Improving)

**Summary Text:**
"Strong improvement! Compliance has increased by 8.3% over the last quarter, showing steady progress across agencies."

**Recommendation:**
"Great work! Focus on maintaining these standards and exploring advanced accessibility features. Share best practices across agencies."

**Visual Cues:**
- All KPI cards: Green background (good status)
- Chart: Upward trending line
- Icon: 📈 with "Improving" label

---

### Scenario 2: Moderate Performance with Growth

**Current Data:**
- Current: 64.2% (Moderate)
- Highest: 72.9%
- Lowest: 55.1%
- Change: +6.8% (↑ Improving)

**Summary Text:**
"Steady progress. Compliance has improved by 6.8% over the last quarter, with positive momentum across most agencies."

**Recommendation:**
"Good progress is evident. Focus on high-impact improvements to reach the 'Excellent' threshold of 80%. Prioritize accessibility and mobile responsiveness issues."

**Visual Cues:**
- Current Score: Yellow background (moderate)
- Chart: Gentle upward slope
- Icon: 📈 highlighted for positive trend

---

### Scenario 3: Declining Performance

**Current Data:**
- Current: 42.1% (Needs Improvement)
- Highest: 61.5%
- Lowest: 38.2%
- Change: -14.3% (↓ Declining)

**Summary Text:**
"Attention needed. Compliance has decreased by 14.3% over the last quarter. Investigation and remediation are recommended."

**Recommendation:**
"Significant opportunity for improvement. Conduct a comprehensive accessibility audit, document barriers, and create a prioritized improvement roadmap. Consider training and resources."

**Visual Cues:**
- Current Score: Red background (alert)
- Lowest Score: Orange background
- Chart: Downward trending line
- Icon: 📉 with "Declining" label

---

### Scenario 4: Stable/Flat Performance

**Current Data:**
- Current: 71.5% (Moderate)
- Highest: 74.2%
- Lowest: 69.1%
- Change: +1.2% (➡️ Stable)

**Summary Text:**
"Stable performance. Compliance scores have remained relatively consistent over this period."

**Recommendation:**
"Consistent performance is good, but consider what initiatives could move the needle. Current efforts aren't significantly improving scores—new strategies may be needed."

**Visual Cues:**
- Change: ➡️ with "Stable" label
- Chart: Relatively flat line
- All scores in similar range

## 💬 Example User Journeys

### Journey 1: Manager Reviewing Weekly Progress
1. Opens dashboard
2. Sees "Last Month" is selected
3. Clicks "Last Quarter" button
4. Reviews KPI cards (current = 82.5%)
5. Scans summary: "Strong improvement, 12%"
6. Glances at chart trend (upward)
7. Clicks download PDF for executive meeting
8. Receives professional report to share

### Journey 2: IT Staff Investigating Issue
1. Notices "Last Day" shows drop to 71%
2. Compares to "Last Month" (still 82%)
3. Sees chart spike down on specific date
4. Uses this date to correlate with recent changes
5. Investigates what deployed that day
6. Downloads PDF to document the incident

### Journey 3: Non-Technical Manager Checking Status
1. Opens report
2. Sees green "Excellent" on current scorecard
3. Reads summary: "Strong improvement"
4. Clicks "Learn more" to understand what it means
5. Reads explanation of compliance
6. Feels confident that team is performing well
7. Downloads PDF to share with director

## ✨ Key Features Highlighted

### For Non-Technical Users
✅ Plain English summary (not jargon)  
✅ Color-coded status indicators  
✅ Expandable educational guide  
✅ Auto-generated recommendations  
✅ Professional PDF for presentations  

### For Technical/Analytical Users
✅ Precise numeric metrics  
✅ Trend direction indicators  
✅ Reference lines on chart (80%, 50% thresholds)  
✅ Interactive tooltips with details  
✅ Date-level granularity  

### For Leadership/Stakeholders
✅ High-level executive summary  
✅ KPI cards for quick scanning  
✅ Trend visualization (improving/declining)  
✅ Actionable recommendations  
✅ Export-ready reports
