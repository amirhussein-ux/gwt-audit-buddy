# Compliance Trend Report - User Guide

## Overview

The Compliance Trend Report is a comprehensive dashboard designed to help government staff, managers, and non-technical stakeholders understand website compliance performance over time.

---

## 📊 Report Components

### 1. **Compliance Trend Report Title & Period**
- Shows the report name and date it was generated
- Displays the selected time period (Last Day, Month, Quarter, or Year)

### 2. **Plain Language Summary**
A human-readable explanation at the top of the report that translates data into simple insights.

**Example summaries:**
- ✅ "Strong improvement! Compliance has increased by 12% over this period."
- ⚠️ "Attention needed. Compliance has decreased by 8% over this period."
- ➡️ "Stable performance. Compliance scores have remained consistent over this period."

### 3. **Key Performance Indicators (KPI Cards)**

Four critical metrics displayed as color-coded cards:

#### **Current Score** (Color-coded Status)
- Shows the most recent compliance score
- Background color indicates performance level:
  - 🟢 **Green** (80%+) = Excellent
  - 🟡 **Yellow** (50-79%) = Moderate
  - 🔴 **Red** (<50%) = Needs Improvement

#### **Highest Score**
- Peak compliance performance during the selected period
- Shows when everything was working optimally
- Green background indicates this is good to aspire to

#### **Lowest Score**
- Minimum compliance performance during the selected period
- Identifies your worst-performing period
- Orange background flags this needs attention

#### **Progress Indicator**
- Percentage change from the first day to the last day of the period
- 📈 **Up arrow** = Improving performance
- 📉 **Down arrow** = Declining performance
- ➡️ **Straight arrow** = Stable (no significant change)

### 4. **Color Legend**
Quick reference showing what different score ranges mean:
- 🟢 80-100% = **Excellent** - Strong digital experience for citizens
- 🟡 50-79% = **Moderate** - Good foundation, room to improve
- 🔴 < 50% = **Needs Improvement** - Significant work required

### 5. **What is Compliance? (Expandable Guide)**
Click "Learn more" to expand an educational section explaining:

- **What is Compliance?**
  - How well your government website meets digital standards
  - Includes accessibility, functionality, and user experience

- **Why Does It Matter?**
  - Ensures citizens can access government services online
  - Works for people with different abilities and technologies
  - Improves experience for all users

- **How to Interpret Scores:**
  - What each range means for user experience
  - Visual breakdown of score tiers

### 6. **Performance Trend Chart**
A line graph showing compliance performance over time.

**Chart Features:**
- **X-axis** = Dates (formatted as "Jan 15" for readability)
- **Y-axis** = Compliance score (0-100%)
- **Blue line** = Average compliance trend
- **Reference lines** (dashed):
  - 🟢 **Green line at 80%** = "Excellent" threshold
  - 🟡 **Orange line at 50%** = Minimum acceptable standard

**Interactive Elements:**
- **Hover over the chart** to see exact scores and dates
- Tooltip shows: Score %, Performance level, and Date
- Chart automatically rotates date labels if there's lots of data

### 7. **Smart Recommendations**
AI-generated suggestions based on current performance:

- **If Score ≥ 80%:** "Great work! Focus on maintaining standards and exploring advanced features."
- **If Score 50-79%:** "Good progress. Prioritize high-impact fixes to reach 80%."
- **If Score < 50%:** "Conduct a full audit and create an improvement roadmap."

## ⏱️ Time Range Filters

Switch between different views to see performance over different periods:

- **Last Day** - See yesterday's compliance
- **Last Month** - 30-day trend
- **Last Quarter** - 90-day trend (default)
- **Last Year** - Full 12-month view

Each filter updates all cards and the chart automatically.

## 📈 Understanding Trends

### What to Look For

**📊 Steady Upward Trend**
- ✅ Indicates successful improvement initiatives
- ✅ Shows improvements are sticking
- 💡 Continue current approach, maybe accelerate

**📊 Stable/Flat Line**
- ⚠️ No progress being made
- ⚠️ Current efforts aren't moving the needle
- 💡 May need new strategies or resources

**📊 Downward Trend**
- 🚨 Performance is deteriorating
- 🚨 Issues may be accumulating faster than fixes
- 💡 Urgent investigation and remediation needed

**📊 Spikes (Sudden Jumps)**
- ✅ Major improvements after a fix
- 🚨 Drops after new features launched (common)

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: What if my score is low?**  
A: It's not about where you start—it's about improving. Track progress and celebrate incremental gains.

**Q: Why did the score drop after I added new features?**  
A: New features might introduce accessibility or usability issues. This is normal and fixable.

**Q: How do I reach 80%?**  
A: Focus on the highest-impact issues first. The recommendations show what areas need work.

**Q: Can I compare agencies?**  
A: This report shows system-wide trends. For agency comparisons, see the Agency Leaderboard.

**Q: How often is this data updated?**  
A: Data is updated daily as audits complete. Check back regularly for fresh insights.

## 🔧 Technical Details

### Data Aggregation
- Scores are averaged across all audited agencies
- Only recent completed audits are included
- Partial data doesn't skew results

### Chart Interpretation
- Each data point = average score for that day
- Line smoothing makes trends easier to see
- Reference lines help identify targets

### PDF Generation
- Captures real-time data
- Includes snapshot of performance at download time
- Multi-page format for comprehensive reporting
