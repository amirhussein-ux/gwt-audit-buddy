## 📈 Metrics Interpretation Guide

### Top Score (92.3%)
**Meaning:** Best performing agency in system  
**Use:** Benchmark of excellence, learning source  
**Action:** Document their practices, share learnings

### Average Score (78.5%)
**Meaning:** Typical system-wide performance  
**Use:** Historical baseline, improvement target  
**Action:** Track changes month-over-month

### Lowest Score (61.2%)
**Meaning:** Agency needing most support  
**Use:** Priority for assistance, improvement focus  
**Action:** Investigate barriers, provide resources

### Elite Count (7 of 10)
**Meaning:** 70% of top agencies exceed excellence  
**Use:** System health indicator  
**Use:** If > 50%, system is healthy; if < 30%, intervention needed

### Performance Gap (31.1%)
**Meaning:** Spread between best and worst  
**Use:** Opportunity indicator, mentorship potential  
**Use:** Gap < 15% = consistent; Gap > 25% = significant variance
